Incident Review Q3 2026

Prepared by the operations team. Three incidents in the quarter, no injuries.

Incident 1 - 14 July 2026, Mannheim (KX-1)

A K-4 Kestrel was grounded mid-route after a lightning strike 9 km away; the 15/30 rule was applied
and the drone waited at a safe landing point. The parcel arrived 41 minutes after the end of its
Standard window, so the late-delivery refund applied.

Incident 2 - 2 August 2026, Rhine crossing near Ludwigshafen
(KX-2)

A K-7 Heron released its payload early over the river bank. Root cause: a timing bug in latch
firmware 3.1.4. The payload was recovered, but damaged. The fleet was updated to latch firmware
3.1.6 within 48 hours; no repeat since.

Incident 3 - 3 September 2026, Heidelberg (near miss)

A K-2 Sparrow came within 20 m of a newly erected construction crane that was missing from the
map. New rule: construction-zone geofences are refreshed every Monday from the city's permit
feed.
