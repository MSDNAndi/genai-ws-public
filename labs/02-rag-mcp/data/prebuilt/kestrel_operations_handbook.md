2. Weather rules

No flight may start when the forecast wind at flight altitude exceeds the model's maximum wind
rating. Lightning: if a strike is detected within 15 km of the route, all drones on that route are
grounded until 30 minutes after the last strike (the 15/30 rule). Below -10 degrees Celsius batteries
must be pre-warmed to at least 15 degrees before launch.

3. Night operations

Night flights (sunset to sunrise) require form NW-17, signed by the shift lead before the first launch
of the night. The maximum altitude at night is 90 m above ground; during the day it is 120 m.

4. Battery policy

A battery whose state of health falls below 82 % is retired and sent to recycling. Batteries in storage
are kept at a 55 % charge. Every battery carries a QR label with its cycle count.
